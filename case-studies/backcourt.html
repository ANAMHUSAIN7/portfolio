Backcourt

An ongoing client account spanning social media, campaign production, tournament coverage, shoot direction, website, branding and performance marketing.

MY ROLEClient management · Creative operations · Social media · Shoot direction · Event and vendor coordination
01 / Project context

More than a branding project

Backcourt is a padel and pickleball club, and the account extended well beyond brand identity. I managed day-to-day client communication and meetings while coordinating social content, creative production, shoots and on-ground activity—including coverage for THE OPEN tournament.

02 / My contribution

Connecting the whole account

I was the day-to-day link between the client, creative team and external vendors. I gathered briefs, translated feedback into clear tasks for designers and creatives, tracked approvals and deliverables, and coordinated photographers, editors and shoot logistics—including court shoots for social media. The scope also included creative posts and stories, website and branding work, and performance marketing coordination.

03 / Delivery approach

From everyday content to live tournament coverage

  1. 01Run regular client meetings, capture briefs and confirm priorities
  2. 02Brief the creative team and coordinate design, posts, stories and website work
  3. 03Align photographers, editors, shoots and court content for social channels
  4. 04Coordinate on-ground production and coverage for THE OPEN tournament
  5. 05Keep vendors, timelines, approvals and performance-marketing activity moving
