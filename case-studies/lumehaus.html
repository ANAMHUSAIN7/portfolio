01 / Project context

Managing a premium design account

LumeHaus is a premium lighting brand bringing together design-led products and Indian craftsmanship. My role grew beyond social media management into broader client servicing and delivery across content, campaigns, product communication and on-ground execution.

I was the day-to-day point of contact for social media across Instagram, Pinterest and Facebook, managing captions, content calendars, publishing and routine reporting. I also supported SEO through articles, contributed to campaign brainstorming and moodboards, and coordinated shoot vendors, timelines and deliverables.

02 / My contribution

From product storytelling to on-ground delivery

My contribution included helping develop product and collection names, supporting campaign ideas and translating them into content requirements and moodboards for the creative team. I coordinated shoot vendors and production timelines, followed up on deliverables, and helped keep client feedback and approvals moving.

For India Design, I also supported on-ground execution by coordinating vendors at the stall. That meant managing practical production details and vendor communication on site, connecting the planning work with the realities of event delivery. This case study focuses on my client, content and operations responsibilities, while crediting overall brand creative direction separately.

  3. 03Coordinate shoot vendors, creative requirements, production timelines, deliverables and approvals
